### 2. Additive/split canonical mechanism records — 16/16 terminal

#### Demography / finance / staffing — legacy 0188

1. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0188-DEMAND-CAPACITY-FORECAST-DESIGN` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. **Key Finding:** robust local demand/capacity forecasting can prevent both unplanned closures and costly empty capacity when child numbers fall. **Problem Review:** demographic decline is spatially uneven; aggregate Land totals are insufficient. **Goal Review:** accessible high-quality childcare at sustainable capacity, not preservation of every existing place. **Mechanism/risks:** needs data → location/capacity decisions → utilisation/access; forecast error can lengthen travel or lock in excess capacity. **Delivery/distribution:** municipal/provider data, rural access, mobility, children with support needs. **Recheck:** utilisation, travel/access, closures/opening days, forecast error, quality and lifecycle cost.

2. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0188-STABLE-FINANCING-STAFF-RATIO-RETENTION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. **Key Finding:** financing that decouples necessary quality/personnel from short-run demographic volatility, together with a better real staff-child ratio, can strengthen continuity, individual support and retention. Current Land demographic/personnel support is an inherited baseline, so only the additional design counts. **Risks:** financing formulas can preserve inefficient capacity or fail to reflect actual attendance/absence; formal staffing keys may overstate available interaction time. **Recheck:** real present staff-child ratio, absences, turnover/vacancies, opening reliability, quality, regional distribution and durable OPEX funding.

3. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0188-ROUND-TABLE-FINANCE-PERSONNEL-STRUCTURE` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. **Key Finding:** structured co-design with municipalities/providers/staff/parents can surface delivery constraints and improve implementability if findings change the final financing/personnel/structure design. The round table itself is **output, not outcome**. Risks: insider dominance, delay and non-binding recommendations. **Recheck:** representation, published problem/data basis, accepted/rejected recommendations with reasons, time to decision and measurable design changes.

#### Administrative workload / digital support — legacy 0190

4. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0190-DOCUMENTATION-DUTY-REDUCTION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. **Key Finding:** removing duplicate/low-value documentation can return staff time to children; deleting information needed for child protection, inclusion/funding chains, handovers, quality assurance or accountability can worsen outcomes. **Goal:** more direct pedagogical time with preserved protection/information functions, not fewer forms per se. **Falsification:** measured staff time saved **and** stable/better protection, handover, funding and quality-data performance; exact duty/function negative-list required before implementation.

5. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0190-DIGITAL-KITA-WORKFLOW-SUPPORT` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Usable interoperable digital support can reduce repeat entry, coordination time and administrative error. Risks: new double documentation, vendor lock-in, privacy/cyber failures and unequal digital capability. **Delivery/boundaries:** data minimisation, role/access controls, offline/fallback and usability with practitioners. **Recheck:** time/entries per process, error rates, user burden, incidents, uptime and actual direct-care time.

6. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0190-KITA-LEADERSHIP-RELIEF` — `REVIEWED_NOT_ASSESSABLE_WITH_EXACT_REASON` · `OPEN` · `NOT_ASSESSABLE`. `Leitungskräfte stärker entlasten` is a legitimate capacity goal but names no separate staffing, deputy-time, admin-pool, process or funding mechanism beyond the preceding documentation/digital clauses. No additional direction may be inferred. Recheck only against a concrete relief design with leadership time, staff supervision, quality and cost indicators.

#### Kindertagespflege — legacy 0191

7. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0191-CHILDMINDER-COMPENSATION-MATERIAL-COST` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Fair, cost-reflective remuneration and material-cost support can stabilise the supply of qualified childminders and preserve parental choice, particularly for smaller/flexible settings. Risks: formula not linked to real costs/quality, uneven municipal treatment or funding places without demand. **Recheck:** provider entry/retention, net remuneration/cost coverage, place availability/use, quality, regional distribution and public cost.

8. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0191-CHILDMINDER-ADVISORY-SUPPORT` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Accessible professional advice can improve quality, compliance, safeguarding and provider retention if advice capacity and competence match actual needs. **Output != Outcome:** advice contacts alone are insufficient. **Recheck:** waiting/access, resolved cases, provider retention, quality/safeguarding findings and geography. Parent `Wunsch- und Wahlrecht` is a rights/distribution guard, not a separate effect unit.

#### All-day care / school-Hort coordination — primary-source gaps

9. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-GAP-P48-ALLDAY-RIGHT-INHERITED-IMPLEMENTATION` — `SOURCE_UNIT_RECLASSIFIED_VERSIONED` · `OPEN` · `MEDIUM`. **Current-baseline correction:** the federal §24(4) SGB VIII right begins with first grade in 2026/27, but Sachsen-Anhalt already provides a broader all-day childcare right, so `Rechtsanspruch ... ab 2026 umsetzen` is not a new rights outcome generated by a future CDU term. Remaining effect may lie in transition funding, interfaces, quality and operational continuity, but those are not specified in this sentence. **Lifecycle:** `INHERITED_LAND_RIGHT + NEW_FEDERAL_PHASE_IN -> IMPLEMENTATION/QUALITY`. Recheck access/coverage, holiday/day hours, staffing, denial/complaints and incremental federal-finance use.

10. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-GAP-P48-KITA-HORT-PRIMARY-SCHOOL-ALLDAY-COORDINATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. **Key Finding:** coordinated school/Hort/all-day structures and rhythmic pedagogical concepts can reduce transition/interface losses and improve coherent learning, play, care and family reliability. Current 2023–2025 Land model-project experience is inherited baseline; Additionality is scaling/quality implementation. **Risks:** schoolification of free time, fragmented responsibilities, staff shortages and long child days. **Recheck:** fixed cooperation structures, joint planning/goals, child/parent experience, handover errors, participation, staff burden, attendance/absence and accessibility.

#### Health / movement / media — restored legacy 0193

11. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0193-HEALTHY-AFFORDABLE-KITA-MEALS` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Nutritious affordable meals can improve food access/diet quality and reduce social exclusion if standards, procurement and affordability are real. Risks: higher fees, waste, low acceptance and procurement without nutritional outcome. **Recheck:** meal quality/standards, price burden/take-up by income, waste, acceptance and relevant health/nutrition indicators without over-attribution.

12. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0193-MOVEMENT-PROMOTION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Regular age-appropriate movement opportunities can support motor development, wellbeing and healthy routines. Conditions: inclusive access, sufficient space/time and no displacement of play/rest. **Recheck:** actual movement opportunity/participation, motor development measures, inclusion and programme quality.

13. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0193-MOTOR-SCREENING` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Regular motor checks can detect support needs earlier **only** if the screening is valid, age-appropriate and linked to available support/medical pathways. Risks: false positives/negatives, labelling, privacy/consent issues and diagnosis without capacity. **Recheck:** test performance, referral/follow-up, uptake, developmental outcomes and unequal classification/access.

14. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0193-AGE-APPROPRIATE-MEDIA-EDUCATION` — `SOURCE_UNIT_RECLASSIFIED_VERSIONED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. **Source Restore:** the final PDF completes the truncated sentence: media education should be age-appropriate and pedagogically accompanied, not mere occupation. **Additionality guard:** media education is already an explicit focus of the binding 2025 Land educational programme. The positive mechanism therefore concerns implementation quality/capability, not inventing media education. Risks: screen-time substitution, commercial/data exposure and poor-quality digital use. **Recheck:** pedagogical quality, staff competence, child media/data-safety skills, access, screen/use patterns and parent/practitioner feedback.

#### Buildings / hours — legacy 0194

15. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0194-NEEDS-BASED-KITA-EXPANSION-RENOVATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Needs-based expansion/renovation can improve building quality, accessibility, heat/climate safety and local capacity, especially rural. Under demographic decline, capacity-blind building can create stranded OPEX/empty places. **Non-compensation:** accessibility, child safety, indoor climate and building-health standards cannot be traded away for utilisation. **Recheck:** utilisation/demand, travel, accessibility, heat/energy/building quality, closure avoidance, lifecycle/OPEX and geography.

16. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0194-FLEXIBLE-OPENING-HOURS` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. More flexible hours can improve work-family compatibility for shift/nonstandard work and reduce access inequality. Without enough staff and child-centred design they can lengthen child days, fragment relationships and worsen staff workload. **Distribution:** shift workers, single parents, rural commute patterns. **Recheck:** demand/use by group, opening reliability, child attendance duration, staff schedules/absence/turnover, quality and parent employment/access.

### 4. Batch-wide #241 system review pp.47–49

- `GOAL_REVIEW`: child development/education, reliable accessible inclusive care, fair participation and family compatibility are end-state spaces; language test, preschool year, merger, documentation reduction, round table, opening hours or all-day network are instruments/intermediate axes.
