### 2. Additive/split canonical mechanism records — 19/19 terminal

#### Administrative modernisation — legacy 0209

1. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0209-CENTRAL-ADMIN-MODERNISATION-GOVERNANCE` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. **Key Finding:** central ownership can coordinate process, IT and responsibility redesign and reduce fragmented standards, but can create a central bottleneck/single-point-of-failure or detach redesign from specialist/local knowledge. **Problem Review:** fragmented ownership must be demonstrated process-by-process. **Goal:** lower-friction accountable administration, not centralisation itself. **Recheck:** decision/deployment times, duplicate standards, local exception handling, service outcomes, staffing/cost and failure concentration.

2. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0209-END-TO-END-DIGITAL-PERMIT-PROCUREMENT` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. End-to-end digital approval/procurement can reduce media breaks, duplicate input and processing error if back-office procedures, evidence, signatures/identity and interfaces are actually integrated. Existing OZG/digital-service programmes are inherited baseline; Additionality is the **full end-to-end specialist process**. **Risks:** merely front-ending paper logic, vendor lock-in, exclusion and cyber outage. **Recheck:** application→decision time, repeat entries/media breaks, errors/appeals, accessibility, uptime and full cost.

3. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0209-APPROVAL-FICTION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. A legally tailored deemed-approval rule can create deadline discipline and reduce delay risk in suitable low-risk/standard cases. It can also deem approval where substantive safety, environmental, neighbour, fiscal or other rights require examination. §42a-type legal architecture means a fiction must be explicitly legislated and tied to complete/determined applications; it is not a universal administrative shortcut. **Recheck:** eligible case classes, time saved, incomplete/complex cases, later revocation/litigation/damage, affected third parties and authority capacity.

4. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0209-RISK-BASED-SAMPLE-CONTROLS` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Replacing exhaustive low-value proof checks with well-designed risk-based samples can reduce public/private verification cost while preserving deterrence. Poor risk models or under-sampling can increase error, fraud, unequal scrutiny or missed protection failures. **Protection:** statutory audit/control duties remain; sampling criteria must be reviewable and non-discriminatory. **Recheck:** hours/cost saved, detected error/fraud, false positives/negatives, recovery/clawback, group/sector selection and outcome quality.

5. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0209-AI-ADMIN-PRECHECK` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. AI can triage/check completeness and support routine pre-review, reducing queue burden when accuracy is demonstrably high. It can scale data/model errors, bias or opacity and cannot silently replace legally required human responsibility. **Current implementation guard:** AI Act/use-case duties, data protection, audit/logging, human override/appeal and fallback. **Recheck:** accuracy by case/group, processing time, override/appeal, adverse rights effects, staff workload, incidents and model drift.

6. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0209-ADMIN-LAW-DIGITAL-COMMUNICATION-ADAPTATION` — `REVIEWED_NOT_ASSESSABLE_WITH_EXACT_REASON` · `OPEN` · `NOT_ASSESSABLE`. `Verwaltungsrecht weiter an digitale Kommunikationswege anpassen` names no provision, communication act, authentication/evidence rule, notice/service regime or legal delta. Better digital communication can reduce friction, but no direction may be assigned before exact law and rights/procedure safeguards are specified.

#### Planning / permitting — legacy 0210

7. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0210-PROCESS-TIME-LIMITS-LEGAL-REVIEW` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Clear processing targets/statutory limits and removal of genuinely redundant steps can reduce queue uncertainty and capital/implementation delay. Arbitrary limits can shift work after approval, weaken participation/evidence/protection or increase denials/litigation. **Goal:** faster lawful high-quality decisions, not lowest calendar time. **Recheck:** authority processing vs applicant pause time, decision quality/errors, appeals/court outcomes, staff load, protection outcomes and project realisation.

8. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0210-BUND-LAENDER-PERMIT-BARRIER-REDUCTION-ADVOCACY` — `REVIEWED_NOT_ASSESSABLE_WITH_EXACT_REASON` · `OPEN` · `NOT_ASSESSABLE`. Generic federal/Länder advocacy to reduce `bureaucratic hurdles` does not identify the rule, actor, protection function or proposed alternative. The source's BauO/LVergabe references are current/inherited measures and must not be double-counted as a future generic reform outcome.

9. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0210-ABOLISH-ASSOCIATION-LITIGATION-ADVOCACY` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Removing some procedural abuse/duplicative challenge routes could shorten finality in narrowly defined cases, but a blanket abolition of environmental association remedies would weaken independent legality/environmental review and is materially constrained by UmwRG/EU/Aarhus access-to-justice obligations. The source frame `ideologisch motivierte Blockaden` is not evidence that covered claims are abusive. **Competence/legal feasibility:** federal/EU multi-level, not Land unilateral. **Recheck:** exact remedy scope, case success/defect-correction rates, time attributable to remedies vs administrative/applicant causes, environmental/third-party outcomes and compliance with higher law.

#### State/municipal organisation — legacy 0211/0212 + missing basis services

10. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0211-TASK-REVIEW-ADMIN-REFORM` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. A real task review before structural reform can identify redundant levels/processes and free scarce staff; pre-set staffing/slimming targets can bias the diagnosis and cut capacity needed for statutory outcomes. **Goal:** fewer redundant transactions with maintained capability/protection, not fewer organisational units. **Recheck:** task/process map, contacts/handovers, processing quality/time, staffing/cost, service reach and statutory failures.

11. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0211-REDUCE-LEGAL-TECHNICAL-OVERSIGHT-REPORTING` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Removing duplicate/low-value oversight/reporting can lower transaction cost. Reducing legal/technical supervision `to a minimum` can weaken legality, equal treatment, safety/protection and detection of local systemic failures. Exact function-by-function negative-list required. **Recheck:** hours/cost saved, complaints/appeals/audit findings, local variance, rights/protection failures and corrective response.

12. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0211-REMOVE-DUPLICATE-RESPONSIBILITIES-MUNICIPAL-AUTONOMY` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Clear single ownership can reduce handoffs and strengthen municipal accountability where tasks/capability fit locally. Removing shared competence can lose scale, specialist capacity or equal-service safeguards and create postcode variance. **Recheck:** handoffs/time, local staffing/expertise, service quality/rights consistency, cost and rural/small-municipality effects.

13. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-GAP-P54-P55-MUNICIPAL-BASE-SERVICES-INTERMUNICIPAL-PLATFORMS` — `SOURCE_UNIT_RECLASSIFIED_VERSIONED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Intermunicipal common services/platforms and reusable standards can reduce duplicate development/procurement and help low-capacity municipalities. Current OZG/BeBaST already provides central reusable components, so Additionality is incremental service scope/use. **Risks:** central lock-in/failure, mismatch with local needs and migration cost. **Recheck:** adoption, procurement/process time, cost per service, interoperability, uptime/recovery, local exceptions and service outcomes.

`0212` remains source-parity `SAME` and terminal `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED · AMBIVALENT · LOW`: centralising delegated-sphere procedures can improve standardisation/scale but can lose local knowledge/access and create central bottlenecks/single points of failure. Recheck by task class, caseload, distance/digital/analogue access, processing quality/time and resilience.

#### Report moratorium — primary-source gap

14. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-GAP-P55-REPORTING-MORATORIUM-SUNSET-JUSTIFICATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Requiring every report duty to show a continued purpose and expire/review can remove obsolete/duplicate data burden. A blanket moratorium/sunset can also erase data required for accountability, public finance, labour/social rights, safety, environment, equality, statistics, EU/federal compliance or programme evaluation. **Problem Review:** `felt burden` is not sufficient evidence that a duty lacks value. **Design guard:** inventory each duty with user/purpose/legal basis/data reuse/risk before removal. **Recheck:** reporting time/cost saved **and** decision/monitoring/audit/protection data quality, legal noncompliance, blind spots and downstream duplicate data requests.

#### Accessibility / participation — legacy 0213

15. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0213-END-TO-END-DIGITAL-ACCESSIBILITY` — `SOURCE_UNIT_RECLASSIFIED_VERSIONED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. End-to-end accessible Land services can reduce exclusion for disabled/older/other users, but accessibility is already a legal/strategic obligation: Additionality is actual conformance/usability across the full transaction. The p55 premise that many procedures will soon be `exclusively digital` creates a **policy-coherence risk** with the p51 analogue-personal fallback and cannot be assumed desirable. **Recheck:** accessibility audits incl. process/UI/documents, successful completion/abandonment by group, complaints, remediation time and analogue-equivalent access where needed.

16. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0213-ASSISTIVE-TECHNOLOGY-SUPPORT` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Supporting assistive technologies can expand independent access if tools interoperate with Land services and are actually affordable/available. Risks: proprietary incompatibility, support gaps and procurement without user benefit. **Recheck:** eligible/user uptake, task completion/independence, compatibility, support time, user feedback and cost.

17. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0213-DIGITAL-PARTICIPATION-FORMATS` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Digital participation can lower time/geographic barriers and widen inputs, but can overrepresent digitally active groups, invite manipulation/low-quality participation or become consultation without response. **Protection:** accessibility, privacy, anti-abuse, analogue/complementary participation and documented feedback. **Recheck:** participant diversity, accessibility, deliberation/abuse metrics, response/design changes and trust/acceptance without claiming causality from participation counts.

#### Digital twins — legacy 0214

18. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0214-DIGITAL-TWIN-PLANNING-SIMULATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. 3D/digital-twin simulation can improve option comparison, spatial conflicts and scenario visibility before expensive lock-in. `realitätsnah` models are not reality: incomplete/stale data, assumptions, optimisation targets and model error can create false precision or bias decisions. **Recheck:** validation/calibration, forecast error, scenarios/uncertainty, decisions actually changed, planning time/cost, environmental/social/economic dimensions and data governance.

19. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0214-DIGITAL-TWIN-TRANSPARENCY-PARTICIPATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Visual models can make alternatives easier to understand and support earlier participation if underlying assumptions/data and non-digital access are transparent. They can also persuade through visual realism while hiding uncertainty or exclude users without digital access. **Recheck:** participant comprehension/diversity, displayed uncertainty/options, accessibility, documented response, data provenance and whether participation changes decisions.
