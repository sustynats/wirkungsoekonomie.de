### 2. Additive/split canonical mechanism records — 19/19 terminal

#### Digital sovereignty — legacy 0197

1. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0197-PROVIDER-DEPENDENCY-DIVERSIFICATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. **Key Finding:** reducing single-provider dependency can improve bargaining power, switching capability and resilience, but regional/vendor diversification by itself does not prove security, interoperability or lower lifetime cost. **Problem Review:** specific current concentration/dependency must be measured by service, data, contract and exit cost, not inferred from provider size/nationality. **Goal:** auditable switching/control capability. **Risks:** fragmented architecture, procurement protectionism, smaller-provider capacity/security gaps. **Recheck:** concentration, exit time/cost, open interfaces/data portability, incident/recovery, lifecycle cost and procurement competition.

2. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0197-SOVEREIGN-EU-GERMAN-CLOUD` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Secure European/German cloud operating models can improve legal/control and dependency management for suitable workloads; geography alone is no security or sovereignty guarantee. Conditions: workload/data classification, cryptographic/key control, auditability, subcontractor/legal exposure, redundancy, portability and tested exit. Current 2026 Land sovereignty strategy is inherited baseline. **Recheck:** availability/incidents, recovery, switching, audit findings, full cost and user/service performance.

3. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0197-CRITICAL-IT-OPEN-SOURCE-MIGRATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Open source can improve inspectability, standards and switching options and reduce some proprietary lock-in. A forced migration without support, maintenance/security capability, interoperability and transition architecture can increase outage/security/cost risks or create dependency on different maintainers/integrators. **Recheck:** actual exit/portability, patch latency, skills/vendor ecosystem, incidents, parallel-run/migration cost and service continuity.

#### Digital administration — legacy 0198

4. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0198-SCALE-STANDARDISE-SUCCESSFUL-DIGITAL-MODEL-PROJECTS` — `SOURCE_UNIT_RECLASSIFIED_VERSIONED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Scaling independently successful reusable services can reduce duplicate development and uneven municipal access. But central reusable OZG components/BeBaST are already a **current Land baseline**; Additionality lies in broader tested reuse, standards and outcomes, not claiming central provision as new. **Risks:** standardising a locally successful but non-generalizable process, central failure correlation. **Recheck:** municipalities/services reused, deployment time/cost, completion/error rates, service quality and local exceptions.

5. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0198-CITIZEN-CONTROLLED-INTERAGENCY-DATA-SHARING` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Reusing lawfully available data can reduce repeated forms and errors. `Bürger sollen selbstbestimmt festlegen` is not a complete legal model: not every interauthority processing rests on consent, and sensitive administrative data require purpose, legal basis, minimisation, logging, access and correction safeguards. **Goal:** fewer repeated submissions with rights-preserving lawful data use. **Recheck:** repeat-data reduction, completion time/errors, unauthorised access/incidents, consent/objection/correction usability and affected vulnerable groups.

6. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0198-AI-AUTOMATED-ADMIN-PROCESSES` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. AI/automation can reduce routine processing effort and improve triage, but can scale error/bias, opacity and contestability problems. **Hindsight/current-law guard:** AI Act requirements/timeline now apply progressively; assessment of a concrete deployment must use its risk class and current law, without pretending this later framework was fully available at programme adoption. **Protection:** human accountability, lawful basis, explainable/contestable decisions, data quality, accessibility, audit/fallback. **Recheck:** processing time/cost, accuracy by group/case, overrides/appeals, adverse rights outcomes, user understanding, incidents and staff workload.

7. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0198-SECURE-WLAN-LAND-ADMINISTRATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Secure WLAN for staff/visitor areas can remove connectivity friction and improve service/access if segmented, managed and accessible. It is enabling infrastructure, not an administration-efficiency outcome. **Recheck:** coverage/availability/use, security incidents, accessibility, support load and whether workflows/services actually improve.

8. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0198-ANALOGUE-PERSONAL-FALLBACK` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Preserving the ability to submit declarations personally when digital channels fail protects access, resilience and non-digital users. **Boundary:** fallback must be practically reachable, not merely legally theoretical. **Recheck:** outages, fallback use/wait/processing parity, rural/age/disability access, complaint/abandonment rates and continuity exercises.

#### Data interoperability / standard portals — legacy 0199

9. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0199-CROSS-STATE-ADMIN-DATA-INTEROPERABILITY` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Lawful interoperable data exchange can reduce search, delay and duplicate evidence across Länder; broad linking can also increase breach, misuse, mission-creep and erroneous-match risk. The programme's crime/social/asylum-misuse examples are **use-case/communication context, not evidence of problem prevalence or a blanket legal basis**. **Delivery:** federal/Länder standards, identity, purpose/legal basis, minimisation, logs, correction and cybersecurity. **Recheck:** successful lawful reuse, delay/error reduction, mismatches, access/complaints/incidents and group-specific rights impacts.

10. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0199-NATIONWIDE-STANDARD-PORTALS-ROUTING` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Common application portals and automatic routing to the responsible authority can reduce search/duplicate interfaces if the same service semantics and specialist-procedure integration work end-to-end. Current OZG/Land shared-component work is inherited baseline. **Output != Outcome:** portal availability alone is insufficient. **Recheck:** end-to-end completion, once-only/repeat inputs, routing errors, processing time, user abandonment/accessibility and local back-office integration.

#### Digital economy / transfer — legacy 0200

11. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0200-DIGITAL-INVESTMENT-SUPPORT-SIMPLIFIED-FUNDING` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Targeted grants/simplified funding can reduce finance/admin barriers to genuinely additional KMU/start-up digital investment. Risks: deadweight, supplier capture, funding already-planned investment and unequal application capacity. **Recheck:** additional private investment, productivity/service/cyber outcomes, take-up by firm size/region, application cost and survival/persistence.

12. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0200-UNIVERSITY-RESEARCH-SME-COOPERATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Better structured research-company cooperation can reduce transfer/search barriers and build regional capability if collaborations solve actual business/research problems. **Recheck:** additional collaborations, IP/knowledge transfer, prototypes/adoption, private leverage, participating SME/region distribution and persistence beyond funding.

13. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0200-TAX-INCENTIVE-JOINT-RD-ADVOCACY` — `REVIEWED_NOT_ASSESSABLE_WITH_EXACT_REASON` · `OPEN` · `NOT_ASSESSABLE`. `steuerliche Anreize` for joint research do not specify tax base, rate, eligibility, federal/Land competence, interaction with existing federal R&D tax support or deadweight control. A Land cannot be credited with an unspecified federal tax outcome. Recheck only against exact legal/fiscal proposal and counterfactual investment.

`0201` remains source-parity `SAME` and terminal `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED · AMBIVALENT · LOW`: Reallabore can enable reversible learning before scale, but the programme's claim that cautious law is `die größte Hürde` is unproven/overstated and `größtmögliche Freiheiten` cannot silently suspend safety, liability, privacy, environment or third-party rights. Pilot learning goals, safeguards, stop/exit rules and transition to ordinary law remain mandatory falsifiers.

`0202` remains source-parity `SAME` and terminal `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED · POSITIVE · MEDIUM`: a capable business-facing CERT can improve incident response/coordination if responsibilities versus federal/sector structures and skilled staffing are clear. CERT capacity is output/capability; avoided cyber losses are later outcomes. Current 2026 Land cybersecurity strategy is inherited baseline for governance.

#### Shared infrastructure / regional IT — legacy 0203

14. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0203-SHARED-CENTRAL-DIGITAL-INFRA-SECURE-CLOUD` — `SOURCE_UNIT_RECLASSIFIED_VERSIONED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Shared base services/cloud can reduce duplicate build and interoperability friction, but centralisation can increase correlated failure and lock-in. Current Land strategy and BeBaST already pursue central/reusable infrastructure; Additionality lies in exact new architecture/services. **Protection:** open standards/portability, redundancy/backups, security operations and clear service ownership. **Recheck:** duplicate-cost reduction, service deployment time, availability/recovery, incidents, exit tests and municipal adoption.

15. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0203-REGIONAL-IT-ECOSYSTEM-VALUE-CHAIN` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. More regional supplier/skills participation can strengthen local know-how/value creation and diversify supply. Preferential sourcing that sacrifices capability/security/value or conflicts with procurement law can reduce welfare/resilience. Regional origin is not a security proxy. **Recheck:** open competitive participation, local skills/jobs/value added, supplier concentration, performance/security and full cost.

#### Connectivity — legacy 0204

16. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0204-FIXED-MOBILE-5G-COVERAGE-GAPS` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Closing actual fixed/mobile gaps and improving reliable 5G/fibre capability, especially for rural areas/schools/business sites, can improve service access and economic/education capability. Coverage is capability/output, not guaranteed jobs/learning. **Recheck:** household/business/school coverage, usable speed/latency/reliability, affordability, use, outage resilience and Stadt-Land distribution.

17. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0204-FREE-PUBLIC-WLAN-CENTRAL-PLACES` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Free secure WLAN can lower access cost for visitors/people without mobile data and improve everyday participation. Benefits depend on useful locations/reliability; public cost, privacy/security, low use and substitution for commercial/mobile access are counter-paths. **Recheck:** unique/useful sessions, target-group access, uptime/security, accessibility, cost per useful connection and local alternative access.

#### Digital capability — legacy 0205

18. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0205-DIGITAL-EDUCATION-CENTRAL-LEARNING-PLATFORM` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Accessible high-quality digital learning/continuing-education and age-appropriate safe learning games can expand learning options. A central platform can also create vendor/content lock-in, poor pedagogy, data/privacy risk and exclusion if devices/support/access are unequal. **Output != Outcome:** platform/accounts are not competence. **Recheck:** access/use by group, learning/skill gains, accessibility, content quality/safety, privacy/cyber incidents and teacher/trainer workload.

19. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0205-MEDIA-PRIVACY-CYBER-LITERACY-ALL-AGES` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Targeted media, privacy and cybersecurity education can improve recognition/avoidance of risks and digital self-efficacy, especially for groups not digitally socialised. Information alone can have weak transfer and widen gaps if only already-engaged users participate. **Recheck:** baseline→post skill/behaviour measures, scam/privacy/cyber practice, reach/retention by age/education/region and accessibility.
