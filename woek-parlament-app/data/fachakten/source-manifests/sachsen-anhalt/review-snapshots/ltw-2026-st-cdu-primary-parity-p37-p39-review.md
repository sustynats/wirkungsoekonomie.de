### 2. Additive/split mechanism records — 34/34 terminal

#### Wildlife compensation

1. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0149-PROTECTED-SPECIES-COMPENSATION-EXPANSION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Compensation for verified rook/beaver losses can reduce concentrated private costs of conservation and improve conflict tolerance. It can create fiscal/moral-hazard or causality disputes if prevention, evidence and eligibility are weak. Goal is fair burden-sharing with maintained species protection, not payout volume. Recheck verified damages, prevention uptake, repeat losses, processing time, distribution and species/conservation state.

2. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0149-LOCAL-DAMAGE-ASSESSMENT-COORDINATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Coordinated district/city damage assessment can improve consistency, evidence and processing if methods are standardised and appealable. Risks are administrative duplication and inconsistent causal attribution. Recheck method concordance, error/dispute rates, processing times and link to prevention/compensation decisions.

#### Innovation / NGT / data

3. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0150-AI-DIGITAL-AGRI-FOREST-SUPPORT` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Targeted AI/digital support can reduce input/labour/information costs and improve precision; it can also increase vendor/data lock-in, capital inequality, cybersecurity risk and rebound/intensification. Recheck actual input/labour/environmental change, cyber/errors, adoption by farm/forest size and lifecycle costs.

4. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0150-AGRI-FOREST-STARTUP-SUPPORT` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Start-up support can diversify solutions and regional capability if it addresses financing/market-entry bottlenecks; deadweight, technology hype and public-risk/private-upside skew are material. Recheck additional firms/products, survival, private leverage, measurable user outcomes and geographic/ownership concentration.

5. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0150-GENOME-EDITING-PRACTICAL-APPLICATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Genome Editing can accelerate traits such as stress resistance and potentially reduce inputs, but impacts are trait/variety/system-specific and include seed/IP/access, ecological and farming-system interactions. Current-law guard: Regulation (EU) 2026/1388 now defines the future NGT pathway; Land effect lies in support/application after applicable rules, not inventing a parallel safety regime. Recheck field resilience/yield/input use, off-target/ecological effects, seed access/cost and distribution.

6. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0150-IPK-PRECURSOR-RESEARCH` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Transparent precursor research at IPK can reduce scientific uncertainty and build regional breeding capability. Research is an evidence/capability output, not guaranteed climate-resilient varieties or lower pesticide/fertiliser use. Recheck reproducibility, field validation, open/usable knowledge, downstream varieties and access/IP conditions.

7. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0150-LAND-FOREST-INFORMATION-PLATFORM-PUBLIC-DATA` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. A consolidated platform plus free region-specific public data can lower information friction and support planning. Conditions: lawful data scope, quality, interoperability, privacy/business confidentiality, equal access and cyber resilience. Crosswalk with 0137/0142 Geobox path; one underlying platform/data capability must not be counted three times. Recheck usage, latency/quality, time saved, user distribution and incidents.

#### Livestock / welfare / permitting

8. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0151-NO-ADDITIONAL-HUSBANDRY-STANDARD-TIGHTENING` — `REVIEWED_NOT_ASSESSABLE_WITH_EXACT_REASON` · `OPEN` · `NOT_ASSESSABLE`. `praxisgerechte Standards` and rejection of `zusätzliche Verschärfungen` do not identify which animal-welfare, environment, building, emission or technical standard is frozen. Without baseline/standard delta one cannot tell whether the path protects planning certainty or blocks beneficial welfare/health/environment improvements. Federal animal-husbandry labelling is a separate information regime, not the missing standard design.

9. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0151-FEDERAL-LIVESTOCK-STRATEGY-ADVOCACY` — `REVIEWED_NOT_ASSESSABLE_WITH_EXACT_REASON` · `OPEN` · `NOT_ASSESSABLE`. A federal `Gesamtstrategie` is a governance goal/advocacy object without a specified mix of welfare, income, investment, emission, market or funding instruments. No Land outcome direction from strategy advocacy alone.

10. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0151-STALL-PERMIT-SIMPLIFICATION-TECHNICAL-STANDARD-MORATORIUM` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Simplifying duplicative permits can speed welfare/modernisation investments; a blanket moratorium on technical standards can lock in inferior welfare, emission, safety or climate performance. Conditions: identify bottleneck rule-by-rule, preserve substantive protection, transition/fallback and legal certainty. Recheck permitting time/cost, actual stall investment, animal health/welfare, emissions, accidents and litigation.

11. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0151-GRAZING-PER-LIVESTOCK-UNIT-SUPPORT` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Well-designed support for extensive grazing can maintain grassland habitats, animal behaviour/welfare and landscape biodiversity. Per-head support can instead encourage stocking/intensity if ecological carrying capacity/outcome conditions are absent. Recheck grazing area/duration/stocking density, species/habitat condition, soil/water, animal health, additionality and farm-income distribution.

#### Marketing

12. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0154-REGIONAL-DIRECT-MARKETING` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Support for regional/direct marketing can increase producer margin, market access and regional value creation if it reaches actual sales bottlenecks. Risks are deadweight and limited access for smaller/new entrants. Recheck additional sales/channels, producer share/margin, beneficiary distribution, transport/packaging and persistence after support.

13. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0154-FRUIT-WINE-TOURISM-MARKETING` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Tourism promotion can diversify income and place value, while creating seasonal traffic/resource pressure and favouring already visible destinations. Recheck incremental visitors/spend, producer/local value share, seasonality, transport/environmental pressure and regional distribution.

#### CO₂ cost / EU-level-playing-field claims

14. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0155-AGRI-FOREST-CO2-COST-RELIEF` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Relief can reduce short-run fuel-cost pressure and carbon-leakage concerns for exposed farms/forestry. Blanket exemption/compensation can weaken the carbon-price signal, shift fiscal burden and delay efficient machinery/fuel/process changes. Current 2026 BEHG corridor is 55–65 €/t; the Land cannot unilaterally rewrite the federal/EU carbon-pricing regime. Recheck net relief by farm type, fuel use/emissions, investment response, leakage evidence, fiscal cost and distribution.

15. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0155-EU-LAW-ONE-TO-ONE-GENERAL-RULE` — `REVIEWED_NOT_ASSESSABLE_WITH_EXACT_REASON` · `OPEN` · `NOT_ASSESSABLE`. `EU-Recht nur 1:1 umsetzen` is a generic rulemaking frame, not an effect-bearing measure. Some EU rules are directly applicable; directives may permit or require national choices; domestic law may pursue separate legitimate protection goals. A specific legal delta and protection-function review is required before judging `gold-plating` removal.

16. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0155-PESTICIDE-FERTILISER-CONDITION-PARITY` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. More harmonised conditions can reduce competitive distortion; lowering national/regional protections to the weakest comparator can increase water/biodiversity/health risks. `same conditions` lacks identified benchmark, substance, crop/soil or exposure baseline. Recheck farm costs/yields, pesticide/nutrient use, residues, water/nitrate, biodiversity and trade/leakage.

17. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0155-LABOUR-SOCIAL-STANDARD-PARITY` — `REVIEWED_NOT_ASSESSABLE_WITH_EXACT_REASON` · `OPEN` · `NOT_ASSESSABLE`. Equal competitive conditions in labour/social standards are a goal without a named legal standard, jurisdiction or proposed change. Cost parity cannot be assumed welfare-neutral; worker rights, wages, safety and enforcement are independent protected outcomes. Recheck only against exact Bund/EU norm and proposed delta.

#### Water / contributions

18. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0156-LANDSCAPE-WATER-RETENTION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. Increasing water retention in the landscape can improve drought resilience, groundwater/soil moisture and flood attenuation when measures are catchment/site-specific. Risks include maladaptive waterlogging, upstream/downstream redistribution and habitat/farm conflicts. Recheck storage/soil moisture/groundwater, low/high-flow, crop/habitat outcomes and spatial distribution.

19. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0156-WATER-MAINTENANCE-CONTRIBUTION-REDESIGN` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. A new contribution basis can improve fairness if it reflects benefit/cost/causation; it can shift burdens regressively or disconnect financing from service/ecological responsibilities. Recheck burden by owner/land-use/region, revenue adequacy, maintenance/ecology outcomes and transaction costs.

20. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0156-WATER-MAINTENANCE-DOCS-CONTRIBUTION-RELIEF` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Reducing duplicative documentation and unjustified charges can lower transaction costs. Relief that removes necessary water/environment/causality information or underfunds maintenance can worsen outcomes. Exact duty/fee negative-list required. Recheck hours/cost saved, data quality, funding gap and water-management outcomes.

#### CAP 2028 / EU budget

21. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0157-ANTI-CAPPING-DEGRESSION-POSITION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Preventing proposed capping/degressivity preserves support for larger farms and may protect capital-intensive viability; it weakens the Commission proposal's redistribution toward smaller/priority farms and changes concentration/additionality. Current baseline is **proposal under negotiation**, not settled law. Recheck support per hectare/farm/worker and by size, farm viability, investment, land-price capitalisation, young/small-farm access and environmental outcomes.

22. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0157-CAP-MFF-BUDGET-PRESERVATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. A stable/high agricultural budget can support income, transition and resilience, but budget volume/sector share is input allocation rather than outcome and has opportunity costs against other EU priorities. Recheck additionality, targeting, income/resilience/environmental results per euro and distribution; Land role is negotiation/advocacy.

#### Fertiliser / red areas

23. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0158-RED-AREA-CAUSAL-DESIGN` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. More causally/locationally accurate designation can reduce false-positive burdens while maintaining nitrate protection. Legal guard is material: BVerwG 24.10.2025 found the previous §13a basis insufficiently determinate; the federal framework is being redesigned. `verursachergerecht` must therefore be translated into legally specified measurement, density, regionalisation and attribution criteria, not individualised exemption by assertion. Recheck legal validity, false-positive/negative classification, nitrate trends, farm burden and protection performance.

24. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0158-SINGLE-FERTILISER-ENFORCEMENT-AUTHORITY` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Consolidating Land enforcement can improve consistency and reduce duplicate interfaces, but can create distance, bottlenecks and single-point-of-failure. Recheck processing/advice/control times, regional access, error/appeal rates, staffing and nitrate/enforcement outcomes.

25. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0158-MEASUREMENT-NETWORK-EXPANSION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. A denser, representative, quality-assured measurement network can improve regional classification, trend detection and legal robustness. More stations alone are not enough: site selection, agricultural influence, QA and transparent methodology matter. Recheck density/representativeness, data quality/latency, classification uncertainty and whether targeting improves while nitrate protection is maintained.

#### EU Nature Restoration Regulation

26. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0159-NRR-REPEAL-ADVOCACY` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Repeal could reduce some compliance/planning constraints and create room for redesign, but it would remove a directly applicable EU framework with binding restoration trajectories before an equivalent replacement is specified, increasing biodiversity/climate/water/resilience risk. Current law also contains flexibility and does **not** itself force individual farmers/private owners to rewet agricultural peatland. Land role is EU/federal advocacy, not unilateral repeal. Recheck actual restoration/land-use burden, ecological state, farm impacts and replacement equivalence before any causal claim.

27. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0159-NRR-REPLACEMENT-FRAMEWORK` — `REVIEWED_NOT_ASSESSABLE_WITH_EXACT_REASON` · `OPEN` · `NOT_ASSESSABLE`. `praxistauglich, fair, finanziell unterlegt` describes design goals but no targets, ecosystems, funding, protection level, monitoring, baseline or legal instrument. It cannot be credited as an alternative until it is specified and benchmarked against current Regulation (EU) 2024/1991.

28. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0159-NO-ADDITIONAL-PROTECTION-USED-AGRI-FOREST-LAND` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Protecting productive land from blanket additional designation can reduce property/production constraints; a categorical no-protection rule can block site-specific measures needed for habitats, water, soils, climate adaptation or legal conservation duties. Recheck area/status/alternative sites, production effect, biodiversity/water/carbon benefit, compensation and legal obligation.
