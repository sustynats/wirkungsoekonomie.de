### 2. Additive/split mechanism records — 20/20 terminal

#### Rural implementation / state capability

1. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0139-EU-FEDERAL-FUNDS-UPTAKE` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. **Problem Review:** underuse or delayed use of suitable external funds can constrain rural investment, but a 100% absorption rate is not itself evidence of an unmet societal need. **Goal Review:** additional, problem-targeted capability/investment, not spending every euro. Full uptake can enable infrastructure/services; it can also reward low-value projects or displace better priorities. Delivery: project pipeline, co-financing, administration. Distribution: municipalities with weaker grant capacity. Recheck: additional completed projects, service/access outcomes, deadweight, co-financing and lifecycle costs.

2. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0139-ALF-CAPABILITY` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Strengthening the Ämter für Landwirtschaft can reduce bottlenecks in advice, implementation and funding administration if concrete staffing/skills/digital-process gaps exist. Output headcount or organisational expansion is not outcome. Recheck processing/advice times, first-time-right, workload, farmer/municipal access, regional coverage and administrative cost.

3. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0139-STATE-LAND-RESERVE-20000HA` — `REVIEWED_NOT_ASSESSABLE_WITH_EXACT_REASON` · `OPEN` · `NOT_ASSESSABLE`. Retaining a 20,000-ha land reserve is a stock decision without stated allocation purpose, access rules, land-quality profile, leasing/sale corridor or opportunity cost. Possible functions include succession, land consolidation, nature/restoration or strategic development, with different directions. Recheck only after purpose/criteria: beneficiaries, soil/nature status, lease/sale terms, additionality, alternative uses and fiscal value.

#### Innovation / automation / skills / NGT / data

4. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0141-DEMO-FARMS-KNOWLEDGE-TRANSFER` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Demonstration/model farms can shorten learning and reduce adoption uncertainty when trials are transparent and representative. Goal is better decision quality/resource performance, not number of demonstrations. Recheck independent uptake, input/labour/emission/resource changes, farm-size/geography distribution, failed as well as successful trials.

5. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0141-LLG-COMPETENCE-CENTRE` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. A stronger LLG research/extension capability can improve testing, evidence transfer and advice if mandate, expertise and interfaces match actual bottlenecks. Risks: organisational relabeling without capability, duplication and supplier/technology capture. Recheck staff/skills, trial quality, advisory use, transfer time and independent evaluation.

6. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0141-ROBOT-DRONE-REGULATORY-BARRIERS` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Removing genuinely unnecessary barriers can enable precision, labour relief and safer operations; blanket deregulation can weaken safety, liability, privacy, noise/nature and airspace controls. `GOAL_NEEDS_REFINEMENT`: lower unnecessary compliance cost **at maintained protection**, not faster adoption as end state. Recheck incidents, input/labour savings, privacy/nature effects, small-farm access, insurance/liability and regulatory exceptions.

7. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0141-AUTONOMOUS-MACHINERY-INFRASTRUCTURE-ACCESS` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Adapted rules/infrastructure can make autonomous machinery interoperable and productive; cyber/GNSS failure, road/public-space safety, liability and infrastructure adaptation costs are material. Recheck operational reliability, incidents, infrastructure cost, utilisation, fallback/manual operation and distribution by farm size/region.

8. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0142-AGRI-TRAINING-CONTINUING-EDUCATION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Updating vocational/continuing training to technical/economic change can improve skills and succession adaptability. Training is output until competencies and employment/business performance change. Recheck enrolment/completion, measured skills, placement/retention, employer demand and access across regions/farm sizes.

9. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0142-NEW-ENTRANT-STARTER-INVESTMENT-SUPPORT` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Starter aid, investment grants and the internship premium can lower entry/capital barriers and support succession, but can capitalise into asset/land prices, generate deadweight or favour applicants already able to co-finance. Recheck additional farm takeovers/start-ups, survival, investment additionality, debt/income, land-price effects and beneficiary distribution.

10. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0142-NGT-CATEGORY1-LEGAL-TREATMENT` — `SOURCE_UNIT_RECLASSIFIED_VERSIONED` · `OPEN` · `MEDIUM`. At programme adoption (13.06.2026) the final EU act had not yet been signed; on 17.06.2026 Regulation (EU) 2026/1388 was adopted and entered into force 16.07.2026, with general application from 17.07.2028. The requested category-1 legal pathway is therefore now substantially an **inherited EU legal baseline/lifecycle**, not a future autonomous Land effect. Until 2028 existing GMO rules still apply. No present Land impact direction from the old advocacy sentence; evaluate later Land implementation/research/advice separately.

11. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0142-NGT-RESEARCH` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. More research can improve evidence on climate resilience, input reduction, agronomic performance and ecological/off-target effects and can reduce decision uncertainty. It does not guarantee lower fertilizer/pesticide use or higher resilience. Conditions: transparent safety/effect testing, variety-specific evidence, seed/access/IP and biodiversity implications. Recheck field performance, input changes, stability under stress, ecological effects and farmer access.

12. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0142-REPORTING-REDUCTION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Reducing duplicate or non-decision-relevant reporting can lower transaction cost. Removing data needed for food safety, environment, animal/plant health, payments or enforcement can worsen control and future targeting. Exact-record negative-list required. Recheck hours/cost saved **and** data completeness, control quality, error/fraud/environmental protection outcomes.

13. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0142-GEOBOX-DATA-POOLING` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. A usable Geobox can reduce duplicate data search/submission and improve location-specific decisions if data are current, interoperable and legally reusable. Risks: stale data, uneven digital access, cybersecurity/vendor dependence and privacy/business-confidentiality errors. Recheck usage, data quality/update latency, time saved, user groups, incidents and decisions supported. Crosswalk with 0137/0150 data-access paths; do not double-count a single underlying platform outcome.

#### Forest policy / resilience

14. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0144-CLIMATE-ADAPTED-FOREST-CONVERSION` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. A broad, site- and scenario-suitable species/structure mix can reduce drought/pest concentration risk and preserve forest functions. Positive direction is conditional, not a guarantee from the label `klimaangepasst`; unsuitable species, genetic narrowing or maladaptive planting can fail. Recheck structure/species/genetic diversity, mortality, regeneration, soil/water, habitat, carbon and adaptive revision after extremes.

15. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0144-WOOD-MATERIAL-ENERGY-USE` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Material wood use can substitute some emission-/resource-intensive products and support a regional bioeconomy; energetic use, harvest intensity and short-lived products have different carbon, air-pollution, biodiversity and opportunity-cost paths. No blanket `wood = climate positive`. Recheck harvest balance, forest carbon/soil/habitat, cascade/material-before-energy share, lifecycle emissions and product life.

16. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0144-PRIVATE-FOREST-MANAGEMENT-INFRA-CAPABILITY` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Funding forest associations, roads, tending and wildfire measures can improve private-forest capability, access and resilience; roads/operations can also fragment habitats, disturb soils and enable higher extraction. Conditions: site-specific need, ecological road standards, additionality and maintenance/OPEX. Recheck treated area, management quality, fire response/access, soil/habitat effects, costs and private-owner distribution.

17. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0144-NO-FEDERAL-FUNDS-FOR-TARGETED-SET-ASIDE` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `LOW`. Avoiding poorly targeted set-aside subsidies can reduce opportunity/fiscal costs; a blanket no-funding rule can remove an instrument where process protection, habitat continuity, deadwood/carbon/water or threatened species benefits are high. Federal advocacy, not direct Land implementation. Recheck site-specific marginal ecological benefit versus production/wood/carbon substitution and compensation cost; hard biodiversity/legal boundaries non-compensable.

18. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-GAP-P39-HARZ-WILDFIRE-BARK-BEETLE-MONITORING` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `MEDIUM`. The final PDF contains a standalone annual Nationalpark-Harz monitoring requirement absent from Release-1. Annual wildfire/bark-beetle monitoring covering deadwood hotspots, water extraction points and road-network condition can improve shared risk diagnosis and preparedness. Monitoring itself is output, not fewer fires or beetle damage. Governance must preserve scientific independence and National Park protection goals. Recheck completeness/quality, publication latency, action triggered, fire response/water/access capability and ecological side effects of subsequent interventions.

#### Hunting / wolf

19. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0147-HUNTING-CADASTRE-DATA` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `POSITIVE` · `LOW`. Providing reliable legally permitted cadastral data can reduce administrative errors and improve hunting-association management. Conditions: data minimisation, accuracy, access control and correction. Recheck completeness/error rate, processing time, disputes, privacy incidents and whether the data improve management rather than merely digitise records.

20. `ST-CDU-PRIMARY-SPLIT-0147-WOLF-MANAGEMENT-ADVOCACY` — `EDITORIAL_V2_PLUS_APPROVED` · `AMBIVALENT` · `MEDIUM`. Better targeted wolf management can reduce recurring livestock conflict where prevention is insufficient and can improve acceptance; excessive/poorly targeted removals can impair conservation status and ecological functions without solving damage. Current-law guard: Directive (EU) 2025/1237 changed the EU protection status, increasing management flexibility, but the Habitats conservation framework remains and national implementation/transposition is decisive. Problem Review must distinguish actual livestock/human-risk evidence from general fear. Recheck population/conservation status, verified damages and prevention, targeted removals, recurrence, illegal kills, livestock protection uptake and distributional costs.

### 3. #241 system review for pp.37–39

- `PROBLEM_REVIEW`: farm viability/cost, rural service/capability, land loss, technology/skills, forest climate/pest/fire risks, small-private-forest capacity and wolf/livestock conflict separated. `Dauerregulierung`, `Freiheit`, `Vorrang` and `innovativste` are frames/goals, not empirical baselines.
- `GOAL_REVIEW`: viable resilient production **and** soil/water/biodiversity/animal/human safety, rural accessibility, adaptive forest functions and lawful data/technology use. Funds uptake, automation, legal simplification, reserve size or wood use are instruments/intermediate axes.
- `DNS_REFERENCE = EXACT_REGISTRY_CROSSWALK_PENDING`; no keyword mapping/causal claim.
- `MATERIAL_OMISSIONS`: quantified bottlenecks/costs, actual uptake/deadweight, soil/land alternative uses, automation safety/cyber/privacy, NGT variety-specific evidence/access, forest site/lifecycle trade-offs, wildfire baseline, wolf damage/prevention/population data.
- `POLICY_COHERENCE`: EU NGT Regulation 2026/1388; BWaldG; Habitats/wolf Directive 2025/1237 and national transposition; land-use/nature/fire/data/safety law. Current inherited law is not future CDU outcome.
- `DELIVERY_FEASIBILITY`: ALF/LLG capability, training/advice, interoperable data, grant administration, forest-owner structures, monitoring quality, wildlife management and cross-level rulemaking.
- `RESOURCE_FINANCING`: grants/start-up, forest roads/care/fire, capability/data systems with additionality/lifecycle/OPEX/opportunity costs.

- `INTERNATIONAL_LEAKAGE`: technology/seed/input/wood markets where material; no automatic leakage label.
