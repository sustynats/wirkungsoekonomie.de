### 1. Current-law / additionality baseline

This page cannot be assessed against a zero baseline:

- **Berlin procurement 2026:** current Berlin thresholds differ by procurement type. For supplies/services, negotiated award without competition is currently possible up to EUR 10,000 net; for construction, direct award is possible up to EUR 50,000 net and freehand award up to EUR 100,000 net. Competition, transparency, equal treatment, economy and documentation remain governing principles. Official: `https://www.berlin.de/vergabeservice/vergabeleitfaden/allgemeines/` and `https://www.berlin.de/vergabeservice/aktuelles/artikel.1630209.php`.
- **Once-Only/NOOTS:** Berlin is already implementing the national Once-Only path; Berlin has ratified/advanced the NOOTS framework and in July 2026 received EUR 4.15m for digital business procedures tied to NOOTS. Official: `https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2025/pressemitteilung.1525299.php`, `https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2025/pressemitteilung.1608649.php`, `https://www.berlin.de/sen/web/presse/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1694690.php`.
- **Transparenzdatenbank:** the core Berlin transparency database is not merely a voluntary nice-to-have. Legal persons seeking Berlin grants must be registered; the database supports public transparency about publicly funded organisations. Official: `https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/service/transparenzdatenbank/artikel.1604844.php` and `https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/service/artikel.1344730.php`.
- **Genehmigungsfiktion:** §42a VwVfG-type fiction is a legally bounded mechanism, not a universal rule that every unprocessed permit automatically becomes valid. It depends on statutory authorization, determinacy and applicable procedural/third-party safeguards. Berlin legal reference: `https://gesetze.berlin.de/bsbe/document/NJRE001592397`.
- **Ausbildungsplatzumlage:** Berlin already enacted the Ausbildungsförderungsgesetz on 26.03.2026 (published 15.04.2026). The current design generally starts the levy at employers with at least **10** socially insured employees and a training ratio below 4.6%; implementation starts via Ausbildungskasse 2027/2028 and includes evaluation/sunset logic. Official: `https://www.berlin.de/sen/arbeit/top-themen/ausbildungsumlage/` and `https://www.berlin.de/sen/arbeit/top-themen/ausbildungsumlage/artikel.1604431.php`.
- **Forschungszulage:** a federal tax-based Forschungszulage already exists under FZulG and has been expanded for 2026. A Berlin source promise using the same label therefore needs an exact instrument/competence distinction before it can be treated as a Land measure. Official BMF: `https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Web/DE/Themen/Steuern/Steuerliche_Themengebiete/Forschungszulage/forschungszulage.html` and `https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Standardartikel/Themen/Steuern/das-aendert-sich-2026.html`.
- **Mutterschutz für Selbständige:** the general MuSchG path is primarily federal; self-employed people are generally outside the standard employee protection architecture, subject to specific exceptions/insurance constellations. Bundestag current debate/background: `https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw03-de-elternschaft-1134292`.
- **SVIK:** Berlin has a current multi-year Länder allocation from the federal Sondervermögen Infrastruktur und Klimaneutralität. It is an infrastructure-investment framework with additionality/eligibility rules, not a generic operating subsidy pot. Berlin/BMF: `https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/presse/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilung.1628289.php`, `https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Standardartikel/Themen/Oeffentliche_Finanzen/SVIK/Laender/investitionen-der-laender.html`.

Hard boundary for the whole page: **administrative simplification, a grant/levy, a larger number of internships or an expenditure allocation is Output/Input, not itself employment, productivity, innovation, training quality or welfare outcome.** Competition, transparency, data protection, legal protection, quality/safety and fiscal additionality are non-compensable guards where material.
