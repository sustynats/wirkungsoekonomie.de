### 1. Current legal/current-policy baseline guards

**Federal/Länder financing is not a blanket `Bund orders, Bund always pays` rule.** Art. 104a GG states the default that Bund and Länder separately bear expenditure arising from their own tasks, with specified exceptions; federal laws creating certain third-party benefit obligations and costs borne by Länder require Bundesrat consent. Art. 84(1) GG also bars federal law from directly assigning tasks to municipalities/municipal associations. Therefore any full-reimbursement proposal must be assessed as a prospective federal constitutional/statutory/fiscal rule change, not as a description of the current universal legal baseline. Official GG: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/art_104a.html`, `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/art_84.html`.

Berlin's tax-enforcement baseline has already moved in 2026: on **24.02.2026** the Senate stated that the Finanzamt für Fahndung und Strafsachen Berlin should be strengthened **personally and structurally** after evaluation. This means the P53 tax-office-strengthening proposal is not a zero-to-one policy invention; any WÖk attribution is incremental/additionality-based. `https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1646345.php`.

Berlin also runs ongoing tax-administration digitalisation/KONSENS and mandatory AI-skills training announced 24.03.2026. `Digitalisierung` cannot therefore be rendered as a new-from-zero state, and efficiency/revenue effects require observed process/compliance outcomes. `https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/presse/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilung.1655491.php`.

**Olympia baseline:** the Senate approved the BERLIN+ concept on **05.05.2026**; the application is therefore an active current policy path, not a hypothetical proposal. The central application project group's approved total budget for 2025–2027 is **up to €6m**. Programme claims of `€10m for the application` and `~€16bn for actual hosting` are not imported as current official facts without a matching scope/accounting basis. Official sources: `https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/media/senat-beschliesst-konzept-zur-olympia-bewerbung-1668153.php`, `https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2025/pressemitteilung.1602515.php`.
