### 1. Current official fiscal baseline / source-claim guards

Berlin's **final** double budget 2026/2027 was adopted by the Abgeordnetenhaus on 18.12.2025. Current official volumes are **€45.528bn for 2026** and **€46.547bn for 2027**; budget-financed investments across both years exceed €12.2bn and the investment ratio is above 13%. This is the current baseline, not the earlier draft values. SenFin: `https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/haushalt/doppelhaushalt/artikel.1236946.php`; Abgeordnetenhaus: `https://www.parlament-berlin.de/Das-Parlament/Arbeitsweise/Haushalt`.

Destatis reports Berlin state debt at **€17,980 per inhabitant at end-2025**, versus a Länder average of **€7,484**. The programme's broad `more than double` debt-per-capita statement is therefore directionally consistent with this current statistic, but Destatis explicitly notes that Berlin's 2025 reduction is materially affected by a sector-classification change for BEN Berlin Energie und Netzholding GmbH. The number is context, not an impact finding. `https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2026/07/PD26_267_713.html`.

The budget itself distinguishes planned (`Soll`) from actual (`Ist`) expenditure. A budget authorization or investment volume is therefore **input/fiscal capacity**, not evidence that infrastructure, education, social or economic outcomes occurred. Berlin Open Data current dataset: `https://daten.berlin.de/datensaetze/doppelhaushalt-2026-2027-1642871`.
