### Current Berlin baseline / additionality / legal guards

1. **BSR is already a public Berlin utility, not a private zero baseline.** Berlin's Beteiligungsdatenbank identifies the Berliner Stadtreinigungsbetriebe as an Anstalt des öffentlichen Rechts with the Land as 100% guarantor; BSR itself describes its waste, street-cleaning and winter-service functions as public-law tasks. References: https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/vermoegen/beteiligungen/beteiligungsdatenbank/index.php/detail/34 and https://www.bsr.de/berichte-und-recht . Therefore U01's `starke öffentliche Stadtreinigung`/`aus einer Hand` language is primarily a governance/coordination target unless a concrete transfer of additional functions is specified; do not render it as creation of public waste management from zero.
2. **Berlin already has a scaled low-barrier bulky-waste/reuse baseline.** BSR reports 616 Kieztage from April 2023 through December 2025, over 260,000 visitors and over 4,500 tonnes, supplementing 14 recycling yards; current 2026 borough Kieztage continue free household bulky-waste/electrical/textile collection and reuse markets. References: https://geschaeftsbericht.bsr.de/2025/nachhaltig-wirtschaften.html and, current example 28.08.2026, https://www.berlin.de/ba-treptow-koepenick/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1701491.php . U03 must therefore be assessed as a frequency/coverage/mobile-service delta, not zero-to-one creation.
3. **Winter-service duties are already legally allocated and cost recovery for substitute action already exists.** Under current Berlin practice, Anlieger generally carry winter-service duty on adjacent sidewalks; BSR already covers roads, cycle facilities, stops, specified public areas and sidewalks without Anlieger. If duties are breached, Ordnungsämter can order Ersatzvornahme at the liable Anlieger's cost; this was actively used in the 2026 ice crisis. References: https://www.berlin.de/ba-reinickendorf/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2025/pressemitteilung.1619984.php , https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/326714/ and https://www.berlin.de/ba-tempelhof-schoeneberg/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1642110.php . Thus U02-A04 is **existing enforcement/cost-recovery baseline**, not a novel effect lever as written.
4. **The 2026 ice crisis demonstrates a genuine coordination/capacity problem but not a simple ownership solution.** Boroughs, Senate, BSR and others required emergency coordination and substitute work; capacity and grit were limiting. Reference: https://www.berlin.de/ba-mitte/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1639853.php . Moreover the 03.02.2026 VG Berlin decision held that a general emergency authorisation for de-icing agents lacked legal basis; StrReinG environmental restrictions remain a hard design constraint: https://www.berlin.de/gerichte/verwaltungsgericht/presse/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1640447.php . Any centralisation must preserve safety, environmental and due-process requirements.
5. **`Berlin räumt auf` already exists.** In April 2026 the citywide rbb 88.8/BSR/nebenan.de action involved schools, associations, neighbourhoods and private participants across all boroughs. Current official examples: https://www.berlin.de/ba-reinickendorf/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1662672.php and https://www.berlin.de/ba-spandau/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1659703.php . U06-A04 is therefore continuation/strengthening, not creation.
6. **Animal-alternative research and 3R are also not zero baseline.** LAGeSo states that many alternative/replacement methods already exist but some animal experiments still cannot currently be dispensed with; Berlin approvals apply 3R. Charité 3R actively develops/researches replacement/reduction/refinement methods, while Bf3R/Bund programmes already fund and validate alternatives nationally. References: https://www.berlin.de/lageso/gesundheit/veterinaerwesen/tierschutz/alternativmethoden/ , https://charite3r.charite.de/ and https://www.bfr.bund.de/fragen-und-antworten/thema/fragen-und-antworten-zu-tierversuchen-alternativmethoden-und-versuchstierzahlen/ .
