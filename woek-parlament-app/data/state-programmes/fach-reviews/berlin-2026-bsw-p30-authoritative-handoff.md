### 1. Current legal/system baseline - material for additionality and competence

The rbb baseline is already materially reformed. Berlin states that the comprehensively revised rbb-Staatsvertrag took effect on 01.01.2024; it added stronger transparency/compliance, professionalised oversight, new duties/liability rules, **capped the Intendant salary and slimmed the management structure**. The Federal Constitutional Court confirmed the Länder's reform position in August 2025:
`https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2025/pressemitteilung.1591610.php`

A further First rbb Amendment Treaty was approved by the Berlin Senate on 02.06.2026 to carry forward the broader public-broadcasting reform:
`https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1676514.php`

Berlin also approved the broader Länder Reformstaatsvertrag path for public broadcasting; this is an **interstate** media-governance architecture, not a field Berlin can redesign unilaterally:
`https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2025/pressemitteilung.1525304.php`

The current household broadcasting contribution remains **€18.36/month** in 2026; contribution level changes require the Länder legal process and are tied to the constitutionally protected functional-financing framework:
`https://www.rundfunkbeitrag.de/presse-und-aktuelles/presse/neues-sommersemester-was-studierende-und-auszubildende-jetzt-zum-rundfunkbeitrag-wissen-sollten`

Current rbb practice also already expands regional live reporting in 2026, so P30's regionality demand must not be assessed against a zero baseline:
`https://www.rbb24.de/panorama/beitrag/2026/05/rbb-staerkt-regionale-live-berichterstattung.html`

The Electronic Media School (ems) is active and 100% owned by rbb; its 15th journalistic traineeship cohort is running and the school reports established training/placement structures:
`https://ems-babelsberg.de/die-ems/`
`https://ems-babelsberg.de/volontariat/`
