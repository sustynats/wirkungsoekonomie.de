### 1. Current Berlin safety baseline / evidence guard

Berlin's 2025 PKS, published 11.03.2026, does **not** support a one-directional `everything is becoming less safe` baseline: total recorded crime fell 6.7% to 502,743 cases and Rohheitsdelikte declined slightly, while some specific risks rose (including firearms crime and recorded knife attacks, with changes in recording methodology also noted). Berlin also already uses geographically targeted safety instruments; from 01.07.2026 seven areas are designated as kriminalitätsbelastete Orte under the current ASOG/kbO framework. Source: `https://www.berlin.de/polizei/polizeimeldungen/fakten-hintergruende/artikel.1078268.php`.

Therefore programme claims about public fear, youth-group violence, social insecurity or `zunehmende Gewaltkriminalität` remain **problem hypotheses/claims by the source**, not accepted causal evidence. For every intervention, separate objective harm, subjective safety, rights burden, displacement, capacity and distribution.
