### Current tax-law / revenue baseline guards

1. **Berlin Gewerbesteuer is currently 410%, including 2026/27.** The adopted 2026/27 Haushaltsgesetz fixes the Gewerbesteuer-Hebesatz at 410%: https://gesetze.berlin.de/bsbe/document/jlr-HGBE2026_2027rahmen/part/X . Berlin's tax FAQ likewise states 410% since 1999: https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/steuern/informationen-fuer-steuerzahler-/faq-steuern/artikel.9362.php . P54's current Berlin baseline is therefore supported.
2. **The tax competence paragraph is overbroad if read as `only Gewerbesteuer`.** Art. 105/106 GG give the Bund broad/competing tax legislation competence, but Länder retain defined own powers (including local consumption/expenditure taxes and the Grunderwerbsteuersatz); revenue from inheritance tax belongs to Länder. Bundestag overview: https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/886622/2d86e5c3efa84a48dcccda0d9ee61347/WD-4-020-22-pdf.pdf and current GG: https://www.bundestag.de/parlament/aufgaben/rechtsgrundlagen/grundgesetz . In the city-state Berlin, municipal tax levers also matter. Do not project `only Gewerbesteuer allows own-law additional revenue` as a settled legal fact.
3. **The 460% Gewerbesteuer proposal is a concrete lever.** Berlin as hebeberechtigte municipality/city-state can set the Hebesatz; the current 410% is the relevant counterfactual. This does not mean the tax base itself is Land-defined; GewStG is federal.
4. **The programme's €400m annual extra-revenue estimate is plausible only as a static order-of-magnitude, not a net forecast.** Official Berlin 2025 gross Gewerbesteuer receipts were about **€3.302bn** at the 410% rate: https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/steuern/steuereinnahmen/2025/artikel.1653706.php . A purely proportional 410→460 static calculation is therefore around €0.40bn before behavioural/base/incidence effects. Preserve the programme estimate as a scenario claim, not guaranteed revenue; current 2026 monthly receipts remain volatile: https://www.berlin.de/sen/finanzen/steuern/steuereinnahmen/artikel.5814.php .
5. **Inheritance/wealth-tax revenue competence is distinct from legislative competence.** Erbschaft-/Schenkungsteuer revenue belongs to Länder, while federal competing legislation governs the tax under current constitutional doctrine: https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/1134488/WD-4-047-25.pdf . `Revenue accrues to Länder` does not mean Berlin can unilaterally set federal inheritance/wealth-tax parameters.
6. **Berlin tax-administration capacity claims have some current evidence, but remain evidence/context rather than separate interventions here.** Berlin Rechnungshof found personnel/organisation deficits and material potential in Betriebsprüfung: https://www.berlin.de/rechnungshof/presse/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilung.1620524.php . Exact U04 reform/enforcement effects still require bounded legal/administrative measures.
