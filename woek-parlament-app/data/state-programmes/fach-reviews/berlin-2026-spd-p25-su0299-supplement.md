This is **not** treated as creating a wholly new substantive right or blanket prohibition. Relevant current rules include eKFV §10 and StVO Anlage 2, Zeichen 239. Therefore the incremental WÖk effect here is primarily **effective prevention/compliance/enforcement or technical implementation of an existing protection boundary**, not the mere existence of the legal rule.
