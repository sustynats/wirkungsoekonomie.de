### 1. Fresh current baseline / hard source guards

The programme text must **not** be rendered as if Ryanair's Berlin base were already closed. Ryanair announced on **24.04.2026** that it *intends* to close its seven-aircraft Berlin base on **24.10.2026**, while continuing to serve Berlin with aircraft based elsewhere. Ryanair itself attributes the decision to BER airport charges and German aviation costs; that causal attribution is an interested-party statement, not independent proof of the full cause chain. Source: `https://corporate.ryanair.com/nachrichten/ryanair-schliesst-ab-oktober-2026-die-berliner-basis-mit-7-flugzeugen/`.

The federal aviation-tax baseline has also changed since the programme/source framing: the Second Act amending the Aviation Tax Act was promulgated **24.06.2026** (BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 182) and the lower rates took effect **01.07.2026**. The Bundestag had adopted the reduction on 21.05.2026. Current tax-policy evaluation therefore must use the reduced post-01.07.2026 baseline, not the pre-reduction rate. Bundestag: `https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw19-de-luftverkehrsteuergesetz-1167350`; BMF process/source: `https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Gesetzestexte/Gesetze_Gesetzesvorhaben/Abteilungen/Abteilung_III/21_Legislaturperiode/2026-03-18-2-Luftverkehrsteuer-G/0-Gesetz.html`.

Berlin is a material but not sole FBB owner: Berlin 37 %, Brandenburg 37 %, Federal Republic 26 %. Therefore Berlin has shareholder/governance influence over FBB, but it cannot unilaterally determine an airline's fleet allocation, federal aviation taxes, all aviation-security/ATC charges or the whole national regulatory framework. FBB: `https://corporate.berlin-airport.de/de/unternehmen-presse/fbb-tochterunternehmen/beteiligungsstruktur.html`.

The airport-wide connectivity baseline is not equivalent to one carrier's base status. BER's summer 2026 schedule listed **80 airlines / 155 destinations / 55 countries**, while Eurowings separately expanded its BER base. Those facts do not negate Ryanair's planned cuts, but they prevent a false `Ryanair base closure = total BER connectivity collapse` inference. FBB: `https://corporate.berlin-airport.de/de/unternehmen-presse/presseportal/pressemitteilungen/2026-03-25-sommerflugplan-2026.html`; Eurowings expansion: `https://corporate.berlin-airport.de/de/unternehmen-presse/presseportal/pressemitteilungen/2025-12-02-eurowings-baut-angebot-am-ber-aus.html`.

Aviation connectivity also has a hard environmental/system boundary. UBA identifies aviation as the most climate-damaging mode of travel in comparison with other transport modes and reports both CO2 and non-CO2 climate effects; airport-area noise/air-pollution effects also remain material. Connectivity/affordability gains therefore cannot automatically compensate climate, noise or local-health burdens. `https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/themen/verkehr/emissionsstandards/luftverkehr`.
