### 1. Current legal/system baseline

The current rbb-Rundfunkrat is already a legally constituted pluralistic oversight body. rbb states that it currently has **32 members**, delegated from societal groups under § 19 rbb-Staatsvertrag; members represent the general public, are independent and not bound by instructions. The council monitors compliance with the programme mandate but may not exercise prior control over individual broadcasts:
`https://www.rbb-online.de/rundfunkrat/startseite/der-rundfunkrat.html`

A formal programme-complaint mechanism already exists. Any person may complain under § 13 rbb-Staatsvertrag; formal complaints allege violation of programme mandate/principles and are decided within the existing Intendant/Rundfunkrat procedure. The council demonstrably decides individual complaints, including granting some and rejecting others:
`https://www.rbb-online.de/rundfunkrat/programmbeschwerden/`
`https://www.rbb-online.de/unternehmen/der_rbb/profil/programmbeschwerden.html`

The 2024 rbb treaty reform and the 2025 Federal Constitutional Court decision remain hard current baselines: Berlin/Brandenburg may shape governance to promote opinion formation/diversity, but **state distance and broadcasting independence are constitutional constraints**:
`https://www.rbb-online.de/unternehmen/presse/presseinformationen/unternehmen/2025/20250821-rbb-begruesst-rechtliche-klarheit-nach-entscheidung-bundesverfassungsgericht-rbb-staatsvertrag.html`

For Berliner Register, the current Berlin administration describes them as documentation/reporting structures for racist, antisemitic, LGBTIQ-hostile and other discriminatory/extreme-right incidents, including low-threshold incidents that may not be reported to police or may not meet criminal-law thresholds. Berlin's current anti-discrimination portal actively lists the Berliner Register as a reporting point:
`https://www.berlin.de/sen/lads/beratung/meldestellen-fuer-diskriminierung-1498255.php`
`https://www.berlin.de/ba-steglitz-zehlendorf/politik-und-verwaltung/beauftragte/integration/artikel.1073997.php`

For Hüseyin Doğru, the relevant EU restrictive measures are real and remain an **EU-level legal act**, not a Berlin sanction. The Council's October 2025 updated listing states its own reasons; Doğru/AFA Medya have separately brought an annulment action before the EU General Court. Therefore the programme's causal/human-rights characterisation is contested legal/political argument, not a WÖk-established fact:
`https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2025/2019/oj/eng`
`https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2025/4627/oj/eng`
