### Current Berlin baseline / additionality guards

1. **Berlin is no longer at a pre-`Baumbegehren` zero baseline.** The Berlin Climate Adaptation Act entered into force on 21.11.2025. It already creates measurable citywide adaptation duties, including street-tree minimum protection/planting, cooling infrastructure, rainwater management and adaptation planning. Official current overview: https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/klimaschutz/anpassung-an-den-klimawandel/klimaanpassungsstrategie-und-klimaanpassungsprogramm/ and law text in GVBl: https://www.berlin.de/sen/justiz/service/gesetze-und-verordnungen/2025/ausgabe-nr-33-vom-20112025-s-541-568.pdf . Any BSW P56 measure must therefore be evaluated as **additional implementation/design delta**, not as creating Berlin adaptation policy from zero.
2. **Tree delivery has measurable current targets and real resource/site constraints.** The law requires tree-stock/spacing and cooling-performance trajectories; current district implementation shows substantial staffing, financing and site needs. Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf, for example, estimates 20,652 plantings by 2040, 8,400 new sites, ~€310m and six additional posts: https://www.berlin.de/ba-charlottenburg-wilmersdorf/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1666240.php . Berlin's own data warn that not every historic tree site is suitable and poor sites can generate high lifecycle maintenance: https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/natur-und-gruen/stadtgruen/daten-und-fakten/stadtbaeume/ . `more trees` is therefore not sufficient outcome evidence.
3. **Kleingärten already gained materially stronger legal protection in 2026, but not all gardens are covered.** The enacted regime protects roughly 56,000 plots on Land-owned ground with narrow conversion exceptions/replacement logic; privately/other-owner land is a different legal situation: https://www.berlin.de/aktuelles/10231348-958090-besserer-schutz-fuer-rund-56000-kleingae.html . A generic `dauerhafter Schutz` proposal needs scope and instrument before an additionality claim.
4. **Entsiegelung is already an active Berlin programme, not a new concept.** A citywide Entsiegelungsprogramm is being developed through end-2026, with net-zero sealing as a policy direction and site/potential prioritisation in progress: https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/umwelt/bodenschutz-und-altlasten/berliner-entsiegelungsprogramm/ and https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/presse/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1653831.php . Friedrichshain-Kreuzberg reports 4,130 m² actually desealed in 2025 and additional 2026 projects: https://www.berlin.de/ba-friedrichshain-kreuzberg/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1692032.php .
5. **Schwammstadt is already current policy/law.** Senate report 09.06.2026 states the Klimaanpassungsgesetz already establishes local retention of rainwater as priority and maps disconnection potential; existing measures include desealing, infiltration, roof/façade greening and irrigation reuse: https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1679421.php . P56's `Leitlinie aller Planungen` cannot be counted as a fresh effect object without an identifiable additional legal/planning delta.
6. **Public drinking-water infrastructure also has a current operating baseline.** Berliner Wasserbetriebe maintain the public drinking-fountain network and announced further additions during the 2026 season: https://www.bwb.de/de/pressemitteilungen_30026.php . `Mehr Trinkwasserstellen` is therefore an expansion object; placement, seasonal availability, hygiene, maintenance and heat-vulnerability coverage are the relevant outcome dimensions.
7. **The 2026 Berlin climate-risk analysis explicitly identifies finance/personnel as major implementation bottlenecks for urban green.** This supports a delivery mechanism for adequate tree/green maintenance funding but does not prove any unspecified budget amount is optimal: https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/_assets/klimaschutz/anpassung-an-den-klimawandel/klimaanpassungsstrategie/klimarisikoanalyse-berlin-2026.pdf .
