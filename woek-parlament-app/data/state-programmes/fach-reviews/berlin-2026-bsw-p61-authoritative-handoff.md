### Current-law / zero-baseline guards

1. **Access to legal advice is not a zero baseline.** Federal Beratungshilfegesetz already provides legal advice/representation for people who cannot afford it; §2 defines advice/representation and §3 permits provision by authorised advisers and, for immediate information/application help, by the Amtsgericht. Berlin currently exposes Beratungshilfe through its service portal and Rechtsantrags-/Informationsstellen; the court procedure itself has no fee and the adviser may charge €15. Sources: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/berathig/__1.html`, `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/berathig/__2.html`, `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/berathig/__3.html`, `https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/326037/`.
2. **Ehrenamtliche Richter/Schöffen already receive statutory compensation.** Berlin's current service/justice information points to compensation for time loss, earnings loss, travel/expenses under the applicable statutory framework; current Berlin administrative-justice information identifies JVEG compensation, including €7/hour for time loss under §16 JVEG and specified earnings-loss rules. Sources: `https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/326928/`, `https://www.berlin.de/ba-pankow/politik-und-verwaltung/aemter/amt-fuer-buergerdienste/ehrenamtliche-richter/merkblatt-ehrenamtliche-richter_innen.pdf`.
