- Berlin's **Multi-Cloud strategy** of 24.02.2026 already aims to reduce dependence on individual external vendors and uses open interfaces/interoperable standards. `https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1646340.php`.
- Berlin already operates an Open Data framework under §13 EGovG Berlin/Open-Data-Verordnung and a current Open Data strategy; the state also decided in June 2026 to move the Open-Source-based Data Hub into durable state-wide operation. `https://www.berlin.de/moderne-verwaltung/e-government/open-data-1513722.php`, `https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1679404.php`.
