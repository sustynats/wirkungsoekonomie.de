### 1. Current legal/system baseline

Berlin already has substantial direct-democracy instruments. Under current Art. 62/63 VvB, popular initiatives can seek laws or other Berlin decisions within Land competence; ordinary Volksbegehren start with 20,000 signatures and require 7% support in the four-month collection phase. Constitutional-change initiatives have higher thresholds. Current Art. 100 VvB requires a two-thirds parliamentary majority for constitutional changes; **only changes to Articles 62/63 additionally require a popular vote**. Thus P32's proposed generalised referenda are real constitutional deltas, not descriptions of the status quo:
`https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/politik/senat/verfassung/artikel.41525.php`
`https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/politik/senat/verfassung/artikel.41498.php`
`https://www.berlin.de/sen/inneres/buerger-und-staat/wahlen-und-abstimmungen/volksinitiative-volksbegehren-volksentscheid/volksbegehren-und-volksentscheid/artikel.24015.php`

The Berliner administration operates under a binding Gemeinsame Geschäftsordnung (GGO I), current stand 02.06.2026; a proposed language/accessibility rule therefore needs to be understood as an administrative communication standard/delta, not as a free-floating language preference:
`https://www.berlin.de/sen/inneres/buerger-und-staat/verfassungs-und-verwaltungsrecht/geschaeftsordnung-der-berliner-verwaltung/ggo-i/artikel.30098.php`

Berlin also has a current, adopted **Landesstrategie für queere Sicherheit und gegen Queerfeindlichkeit** (Senate decision 24.03.2026), based on an existing violence/discrimination baseline and community participation. After the July 2026 attack on Berlin CSD, a special Round Table met on 26.08.2026 on concrete protection measures. Therefore P32's queer-safety proposal is not evaluated against a zero-policy baseline:
`https://www.berlin.de/sen/lads/schwerpunkte/lsbti/artikel.1430848.php`
`https://www.berlin.de/sen/asgiva/presse/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1707319.php`
