### Fresh current baseline guards

- Terminservicestellen/116117 are existing federal/self-government infrastructure, not a new state intervention. KBV currently states a four-week appointment ceiling for eligible TSS requests and two weeks for psychotherapeutic acute treatment; the 2025 evaluation reports about 98% of booked/eligible cases as timely. The operational 35-day reporting threshold reflects one week for mediation plus up to four weeks waiting and therefore must not be confused with a new P29 policy effect: https://www.kbv.de/infothek/zahlen-und-fakten/gesundheitsdaten/statistik-terminservicestellen and https://www.kbv.de/psychotherapie
- Telemedicine is also an inherited M-V development path. The official Gesundheitskommission has had a telemedicine/digitalisation implementation mandate since 29.03.2023, including platform, legal/financial/organisational analysis and hospital telemedical networking; later M-V target documents call for telemedical care pathways and explicit limits where physical care remains necessary. Sources: https://www.regierung-mv.de/Landesregierung/sm/gesundheit/Expertenkommission-zur-Weiterentwicklung-des-Gesundheitswesens/ and https://www.regierung-mv.de/static/Regierungsportal/Ministerium%20f%C3%BCr%20Soziales%2C%20Integration%20und%20Gleichstellung/Inhalte/3_%20Arbeitsauftrag_Digitalisierung_Telemedizin.pdf
- Pharmacy remuneration is federally regulated and is currently being reformed. The BMG states on 22.05.2026 that an ApoVWG plus a parallel ordinance track addresses pharmacy remuneration, with a negotiated adjustment solution and a contemplated package fixed fee increase; AMPreisV currently contains the federal pharmacy markup architecture. Thus P29 is federal advocacy, not a direct M-V price-setting lever: https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/bundestag-beschliesst-apothekenreform-pm-22-05-2026 and https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ampreisv/__3.html
- Recognition of foreign academic/health-profession qualifications is a real Land administrative path: M-V LAGuS/LPH is the competent recognition/equivalence authority for foreign qualifications in academic health and health professions: https://www.lagus.mv-regierung.de/LPH/
- Scope expansion/task sharing is not a blank legal slate. The federal `Gesetz zur Befugniserweiterung und Entbürokratisierung in der Pflege` entered into force 01.01.2026; specific services are to be concretised by self-government contracts, and extended healing competencies already exist for defined training/study paths. Sources: https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/service/gesetze-und-verordnungen/detail/gesetz-befugniserweiterung-entbuerokratisierung-pflege and https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/themen/gesundheitswesen/gesundheitsberufe/pflegefachperson
