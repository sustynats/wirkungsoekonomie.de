### Current legal/policy baseline guards

- M-V already has a current Tariftreue-/Vergaberechts baseline: the `Tariftreue- und Vergabegesetz Mecklenburg-Vorpommern (TVgG M-V)` entered into force 01.01.2024; the VgMinArbV has applied since 15.05.2024 and the MinArbBV since 31.05.2025; official page also records 2026 amendments. Thus the programme’s procurement language is continuation/implementation of an existing framework, not creation from zero: `https://www.regierung-mv.de/Landesregierung/wm/Wirtschaft/%C3%96ffentliches-Auftragswesen/`.
- Works-council elections/activity are governed by federal Betriebsverfassungsrecht; §119 BetrVG criminalises, among other things, obstruction/influence of a works-council election and obstruction of works-council activity: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__119.html`. A Land election programme therefore cannot be rendered as if it directly rewrote this federal protection without a specified Land lever.
- Current §3 ArbZG provides an eight-hour working-day rule with extension to ten hours only subject to the statutory average-compensation condition: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/arbzg/__3.html`. The P23 “Achtstundentag” sentence is federal-policy advocacy, not direct Land implementation.
- The LAGuS is the Land’s upper authority whose Arbeitsschutz division monitors compliance with occupational-safety laws; it has regional Arbeitsschutz offices and medical occupational-safety functions. Baseline: `https://www.lagus.mv-regierung.de/Services/Gebaerdensprache/`, `https://www.lagus.mv-regierung.de/Arbeitsschutz/Standorte-und-Kontakt/`, `https://www.lagus.mv-regierung.de/Arbeitsschutz/Arbeitsmedizin/`.
- The current Land publicly describes the Zukunftsbündnis M-V as cooperation with economy, trade unions and municipalities; this is an existing governance architecture rather than a zero-state invention: `https://www.regierung-mv.de/Landesregierung/stk/Neujahrsempfang-2026/`.
