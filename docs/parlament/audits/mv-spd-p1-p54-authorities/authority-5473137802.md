### Current primary legal/policy baselines used only as context/boundary

- Federal WaffG already requires reliability/personal suitability for weapons permits and regular rechecks; §5 includes statutory unreliability grounds and security-authority queries, while §41 permits individual weapons prohibitions under statutory conditions. Any `Entwaffnung` Fach is therefore bounded by exact legal triggers, evidence, proportionality and review; no blanket political-opinion disarmament is inferred: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/waffg_2002/__4.html, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/waffg_2002/__5.html, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/waffg_2002/__41.html
- For **Beamte**, BeamtStG §33 already requires commitment to and defence of the freiheitlich-demokratische Grundordnung. At the same time, GG Art. 33 protects equal access based on aptitude/qualification/performance and protects against disadvantage due to religious/worldview affiliation; GG Art. 3 also protects equality and political/religious convictions. The programme phrase `nicht die Werte ... teilen / nicht staatstreu` is therefore **not itself an operational legal criterion** and the source also covers `Angestellte`, whose legal path is not identical to Beamtenstatusrecht: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/beamtstg/__33.html, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/art_33.html, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/art_3.html
- MV's Landesprogramm `Demokratie und Toleranz gemeinsam stärken!` is an existing programme since 2006, currently combining prevention/integration/intervention and an existing counselling network including five Regionalzentren, LOBBI victim counselling, JUMP exit/distancing work and DIA.MV. Current official financing information states roughly EUR 4.53m annually across ESF/Bund/Land sources. The P42 proposal is therefore **continuation of an inherited service/funding architecture**, not creation from zero: https://www.regierung-mv.de/Landesregierung/wkm/Foerderungen/Europaeische-Fonds/Landesprogramm-Demokratie-und-Toleranz/

Programme-authored claims about threat severity, causal success or institutional benefit remain source claims, not external outcome evidence.
