# Red Team

The red team challenges a candidate recommendation with its strongest counterargument, weakest assumption and the most relevant missing-evidence question.  Bounded AI may draft these three items for non-critical cases; a human review is mandatory before `READY_FOR_APPROVAL`.

Allowed outcomes are `PASS`, `REVISE`, `MORE_EVIDENCE` and `METHODOLOGY_REVIEW`.  A pass is recorded separately from the recommendation and does not publish it.
